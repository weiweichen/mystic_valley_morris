What we do

Morris dancing, creatively done

Mystic Vallye Morris is a Cotswold style Morris side dancing and playing new interpretations of English folk traditions. We dance with bells on, clashing sticks and waving handkies.

We are a collective of dancers, musicians, and artists striving to create an inclusive space full of creative experssion and community.

The Side

Who's dancing

A quick word on kit, roles, and the people who make the noise.

The Squire

Leads the side, and keeps everyone roughly in time.

The Foreperson/Practice Lead

Teaches new dances, runs practice night and calls the dances.

The Creative Lead

In charge of the costume and puppet design. Keeps the crowd back, keeps the mood up, and is allowed to break every rule the rest of us follow. Currently held by Fiona Rogers.

The Musicians

Fiddle, cello, and a melodeon when someone remembers it. They set the pace and carry the dancers, musically.

The Dancers

The side itself. Dance in sets of six or eight or twelve or ..., keeping the figures tight and the sticks or hankies in time with whoever's next to them.

The Puppeteers

Our processional dancing beasts. An owl, a horse, and a luna moss lead the way, lurk at the edge of a set greeting the audiences. ...

Our kit: red tunic, black shirt, black breeches, purple socks, bell pads, rainbow ribbons, and a flower crown.
